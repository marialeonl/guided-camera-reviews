Case study 03 · Learning tool

Rise, a learning tool

UX/UI Designer See the dashboard↓

UX research · Visual identity · Web app · Internal project · 2023

Mission

An empowering learning platform that offers opportunities for education and skill development.

The challenge

People want to keep learning at work, but they don’t have a clear career path or know where to invest their time.

What I designed

  • Research with interviews, a survey and a card sort, to understand how people learn and grow at work
  • Three personas, and a problem statement built around the mid-level professional
  • The information architecture, from sign-up and onboarding to goals, career, feedback and community
  • The brand: logo and its animation, colours, typography and applications
  • The dashboard: goals, progress, ranking and recommended skills

What it offers

One place for goals, progress and recommendations, a career branch built around a skill matrix and study topics, and a brand to grow on.

02.

Discover

02.

What people told us

Interviews and a survey I ran inside the company, about how people study and grow in their careers.

“It is very important to study and look for spaces to continue learning”
72%

say studying is very important for their career development

46%

study one to three times a week

5%

don’t study

“Employees don’t have a clear career path”
  • They want to know where to invest their time to improve professionally.
  • They are looking for topics that help them prepare for different competitions.
  • It is difficult to identify the route they want to follow when they are not clear about the subject.
  • “It is not to leave them alone and learn by themselves, but to guide them so that they know how to study”
“Need to practice the knowledge learned”
  • They practice their knowledge after learning it: it is important to apply it in real projects.
  • It is important to apply the concepts they are learning at work.
  • Study the subject and do the exercises conscientiously until you can do it.
“Understand the customer optimally”
  • Facilitates relationship with customers.
  • I want to have a stronger communication with the customer and understand what they want and need.
  • I would like to learn about customer needs to learn more about it.

03.

Define

03.

Three people, one need

Personas along the career: junior, mid-level and senior.

P

Paola

Junior role

Paola is a tester who has been working at the company for 6 months. She has a technical role and wants to continue learning to discover her career path.

M

Miguel

Senior role

Miguel is an AM who has been working at the company for 13 years. He is a consultant and wants to guide his team members to fully achieve the goals established.

L

Luis

Mid-level role · Development

User scenario

Luis is a developer who has been working for 2 years. He is an engineer and wants to develop his technical and soft skills through personalized learning.

User needs

As a mid-level person, I want to develop my technical and soft skills, to improve my professional career and contribute to the growth of my company’s project.

Opportunities

  • Certifications and courses
  • Lead in projects
  • Being a career coach

Pain points

  • Have an idea of the topics to study
  • Lack of time to study
  • Challenges in team management

03.

The problem, and the answer

Problem statement

Luis is a mid-level worker who needs to develop his technical and soft skills because he needs to advance in his professional career and contribute to the growth of the company.

Solution

A learning platform that empowers users to create and set specific learning goals while offering continuous tracking and recommendations.

Additionally, data visualization tools that provide leaders with clear insights into the users’ progress, making it easy to track improvements over time.

04.

The solution

04.

From insight to design

Each decision traces back to something people told us.

  1. We heard

    “Employees don’t have a clear career path”

    We decided

    Career gets its own branch, with a career plan, study topics and a skill matrix.

    Where you’ll find it

    My career · Skill matrix

  2. We heard

    They want to know where to invest their time.

    We decided

    Skills are recommended from the person’s career, on the home page.

    Where you’ll find it

    Recommended skills · Study recommendations

  3. We heard

    They need guidance on how to study, and a way to practise what they learn.

    We decided

    Learning becomes goals with steps, a deadline and visible progress.

    Where you’ll find it

    Set goals · Progress rings

  4. We heard

    Senior people want to guide their teams to reach their goals.

    We decided

    Progress is shown over time, so leaders can follow improvement month by month.

    Where you’ll find it

    Goals over six months · My team
