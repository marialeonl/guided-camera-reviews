01.

The starting point

01.

One tap target, and little else

A warning arrived about accessibility failures in the video feed. With VoiceOver or TalkBack, people got trapped in a video and couldn’t move on. There were no controls to pause, mute or skip a video: only a “Botón de pausa” target on the centre of the video.

  1. A

    Controls. With VoiceOver or TalkBack on, controls appear to navigate the feed.

  2. B

    Reading. Reworded labels and a defined reading order for every element.

02.

How it works

02.

The flow

The video starts muted. One tap turns the sound on, and the controls stay put as the feed moves.

The navigation controls don’t move when the video scrolls.

02.

Decisions behind it

  • 1

    Controls only when a screen reader is on

    The bar appears with VoiceOver or TalkBack, and replaces the old centre tap.

  • 2

    Sound stays in the user’s hands

    The first video starts muted. A sound button lets people turn audio on or off so the video never talks over the screen reader’s voice.

  • 3

    Always in the same place

    The bar doesn’t move when the video scrolls, so the controls are where the user left them.

  • 4

    Play wins over sound

    If someone pauses the first video before turning the sound on, play takes priority and the sound control hides. Coming back restores the previous state.

  • 5

    No dead ends

    On the first video there is nothing behind it, so the previous button shows as disabled.

  • 6

    A safe way out

    The player always has a safe exit, so nobody gets trapped in a video again.

02.

Use cases

Each behaviour, one screen at a time.

First video

  • A. It plays without sound. A tap turns the sound on.
  • B. With no video behind it, the previous button appears disabled.

Sound on and off

A tap switches the sound, so people can keep the video from talking over their screen reader.

Play and pause

A tap pauses the video, and the button changes to play.

Coupon

The coupon has two nested actions: open its modal, or close the widget.

If the sound was never turned on

On the first video, if the user pauses without having turned the sound on, play takes priority and the sound prompt hides. When they come back, the previous state returns.

In the app

The shipped feed

In production the controls name what they act on: “Pausar video”, “Video anterior” and “Video siguiente”.

Labels, status messages and descriptions were written with the Content team for a consistent, inclusive tone. Then they were validated with accessibility experts and tested with VoiceOver and TalkBack in critical scenarios.
